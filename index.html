What did the validation reveal?

The NB-Gamma model achieved an overall A/E ratio of approximately 1.006 on the test dataset, indicating good aggregate calibration.

However, area-level A/E ratios varied considerably. Areas A and C had ratios above 1.5, while Area B had a ratio close to 0.51.

This highlights why evaluating a pricing model at an overall level is not enough. Segment-level validation and the impact of large claims also need to be considered.
